app/test_datasets/synthetic/ — synthetic patients (from create_synthetic_patients.py)

  Each synthetic patient is generated by drawing all ~15,000 features independently from the real per-class, per-feature distribution:
  N(class_mean_f, class_std_f) clipped to [-4.5, 4.5]. This produces realistic individual patient variation across all 4 modalities. Sample IDs
  are SYN.BRCA.BL.001, SYN.COAD.CMS1.001, etc.

  synthetic_coad_metadata.csv  reference only

Why synthetic patients are still worth keeping alongside real TCGA files: The real TCGA files show the model scoring 85% on held-out actual patients — that's your honest performance number. The synthetic files let you demonstrate the model on "brand new patients it has never seen" and explain why it makes each prediction (the mrna_, mirna_, methy_, cnv_ feature prefixes show exactly which omics modality drove the call). Together they answer two different questions: "Does it work on real data?" (TCGA) and "Can it generalise to a new patient walking in tomorrow?" (synthetic).
